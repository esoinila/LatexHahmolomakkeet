\textbf{Site: Underground Repository of Knowledge (Giza)}

\textbf{Overview:}  
A vast, hidden library beneath the Giza plateau, filled with Atlantean knowledge and secrets. Toth, the ancient sage, is trapped here and seeks release.

\textbf{Portal:}  
A solid granite door, quantum-locked. Requires three keys, three batteries, and three ancient messages (rites) to open. Major social media funding success required.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Arcana/Investigation roll (DC 17) to find the activation glyphs and hidden entrance.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Solve a riddle about "truth and memory" to unlock the door after powering it. Test of knowledge: players must recite at least three ancient messages/rites.

\textbf{Success:}  
Access to the Atlantean library, blueprints, and prophecies. Toth appears and asks the players to build a new pyramid worthy of Atlantis. Escape sequence begins as the site destabilizes.

\textbf{Failure:}  
Door remains inert, or triggers a psychic defense (visions, confusion).

\textbf{Item Drops:}  
Atlantean navigation crystal (helps find other portals), ancient battery (if not already collected).

\textbf{Revelations:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The final threshold to the Architect's wisdom demands the convergence of three radiant keys and three vessels of ancestral energy, united with the sacred whispers of three ancient lands.
  \item Only those who carry the echoes of the triple rites, those who have listened to the wisdom of the ancients across the veils, shall be deemed worthy to enter the heart of the library.
  \item The enlightened one, Toth, awaits beyond the final gate, but the stone mountain, the vessel of cosmic power, must be reborn before the age of true knowledge can dawn.
\end{itemize}
