\section*{Site: Underground Repository of Knowledge (Giza)}

\textbf{Overview:}  
A vast, hidden library beneath the Giza plateau, filled with Atlantean knowledge and secrets.

\textbf{Portal:}  
A solid granite door, quantum-locked. Requires a major social media funding success to open.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Arcana/Investigation roll (DC 17) to find the activation glyphs and hidden entrance.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Solve a riddle about "truth and memory" to unlock the door after powering it.

\textbf{Success:}  
Access to the Atlantean library, blueprints, and prophecies. Gain a key for long-distance portals.

\textbf{Failure:}  
Door remains inert, or triggers a psychic defense (visions, confusion).

\textbf{Revelation:}  
Prophecy about the "world gates" and a warning about power limits.
