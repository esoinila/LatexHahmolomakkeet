\textbf{Site: Builders' Quarters}

\textbf{Overview:}  
Ruins of the workers' village, filled with tools and half-finished stones.

\textbf{Portal:}  
A stone slab in a foreman's hut, powered by teamwork and Kate/Karl's energy.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Teamwork/Engineering roll (DC 12) to find the slab and hidden mechanism.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Reconstruct a miniature pyramid puzzle using found blocks.

\textbf{Success:}  
Slab opens, revealing a blueprint for Atlantean tech and a clue to the next site.

\textbf{Failure:}  
Collapse hazard or angry spirit of a builder.

\textbf{Revelations:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Where the royal star rests, wisdom awaits.
  \item From the deep wells of time, the conduits of power must be gathered. Three lights to ignite the path, three sigils to unlock the way.
  \item Beyond the veils of worlds, where stone remembers the stars, lies the heart of wisdom. Seek the Sunken Heart beneath the sands, and the voice of the Architect shall guide you.
\end{itemize}
