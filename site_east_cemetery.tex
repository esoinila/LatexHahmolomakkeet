\textbf{Site: Eastern Cemetery}

\textbf{Overview:}  
A dense field of tombs east of the Great Pyramid. Rumors speak of a hidden passage beneath the stones.

\textbf{Portal:}  
A weathered tombstone engraved with the Flower of Life (Atlantean symbol). Requires activation by Kate/Karl with enough energy (fan donations).

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Observation/Tech roll (DC 14) or use of a special gizmo reveals faint glowing lines on the tombstone.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Players must press the correct sequence of symbols (hinted by faded hieroglyphs nearby).

\textbf{Success:}  
A stone staircase descends into a crypt. Inside:  
- A holographic message from Thoth: "Welcome, seekers. The wisdom of Atlantis awaits those who prove worthy."  
- Ancient tech: a crystal lens (lets players see hidden inscriptions elsewhere).

\textbf{Failure:}  
A trap triggers (sand fills the area, minor damage, or time lost).

\textbf{Revelations:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Follow the elongated shadow cast by the stone beast at the first light of day, for it reveals a passage to deeper truths.
  \item The great gate awaits the convergence of three luminous keys and three vessels of ancestral power, united with the echoes of three sacred whispers from forgotten lands.
  \item The city submerged, where the deep currents hold the ancient wisdom, must be found. Its heart guides the way to the architect's final repository beneath the sands.
\end{itemize}
