\section*{Site: Inka Stone Door (Andes)}

\textbf{Overview:}  
A massive stone door carved into a cliff near Lake Titicaca, known as the "Gate of the Gods."

\textbf{Portal:}  
Quantum-locked stone door, requires social media energy and a portal key.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Survival/History roll (DC 15) to find the door and avoid local authorities.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Survive the altitude, power the door, and solve a sun alignment puzzle.

\textbf{Success:}  
Vision of Atlantis and a "passport" glyph for future travel.

\textbf{Failure:}  
Authorities or secret organizations intervene.

\textbf{Item Drops:}  
Atlantean key, Inka battery, navigation crystal.

\textbf{Revelations:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Where the tiered mountain reaches for the heavens, the ancient gods retain their memory. Seek the echoes of the first city in the land of dust.
  \item The path to ultimate knowledge is sealed until three luminous keys and three vessels of ancestral energy are gathered from across the scattered domains, and the whispers of the three sacred rites are heard.
  \item A vision granted by the ancient ones reveals a hidden passage within the keepers' archive, a gateway shrouded in the guise of collected history.
\end{itemize}
