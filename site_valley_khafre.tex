\section*{Site: Valley Temple of Khafre}

\textbf{Overview:}  
A massive stone temple near the Sphinx, filled with columns and shadows.

\textbf{Portal:}  
A hidden door behind a statue of Anubis, marked with Atlantean script.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Stealth/Investigation roll (DC 15) to find the door.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Players must recite an ancient phrase (found in a previous site) or solve a riddle.

\textbf{Success:}  
Door opens to a chamber with a floating crystal.  
- Message: "Balance is the key to eternity."  
- Tech: a balance rod (can weigh truth/lies once per session).

\textbf{Failure:}  
Statue animates (guardian encounter).

\textbf{Revelation:}  
Cryptic map to the Sphinx's secret.
