\section*{Site: Valley Temple of Khafre}

\textbf{Overview:}  
A massive stone temple near the Sphinx, filled with columns and shadows.

\textbf{Portal:}  
A hidden door behind a statue of Anubis, marked with Atlantean script.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Stealth/Investigation roll (DC 15) to find the door.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Players must recite an ancient phrase (found in a previous site) or solve a riddle.

\textbf{Success:}  
Door opens to a chamber with a floating crystal.  
- Message: "Balance is the key to eternity."  
- Tech: a balance rod (can weigh truth/lies once per session).

\textbf{Failure:}  
Statue animates (guardian encounter).

\textbf{Revelations:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The path to wisdom lies in the shadow of the great stone beast, where ancient eyes have witnessed the turning of ages.
  \item Three sparks of light and three vessels of the deep are required to awaken the final gate and reveal the architect's ultimate truth.
  \item Seek the heart that sleeps beneath the shifting sands, for there the echoes of the lost city will guide you to the hidden repository.
\end{itemize}
