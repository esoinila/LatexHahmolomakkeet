\textbf{Site: Queen Hetepheres' Tombs}

\textbf{Overview:}  
A secluded area north of the Eastern Cemetery, marked by ancient boat pits.

\textbf{Portal:}  
A stone sarcophagus with Atlantean runes. Needs power from Kate/Karl.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
History/Arcana roll (DC 13) reveals the runes are a code.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Players must align the sarcophagus lid to match the runes' pattern.

\textbf{Success:}  
A hidden chamber opens.  
- Message from an Atlantean priestess: "The river of stars flows beneath your feet."  
- Tech: a starmap projector (reveals secret paths on the Giza plateau).

\textbf{Failure:}  
A spectral guardian appears (combat or negotiation).

\textbf{Revelations:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The celestial currents guide those who seek the hidden knowledge. Follow the river of stars that flows unseen beneath the earth to uncover the next truth.
  \item The path to the heart of ancient power requires the convergence of three luminous keys and three vessels charged with the spark of creation, scattered across the forgotten lands.
  \item The echoes of the city that sleeps beneath the waves whisper of a final passage. Find its submerged heart, and the architect's ultimate library will be within reach.
\end{itemize}
