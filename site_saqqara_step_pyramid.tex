\section*{Site: Step Pyramid Complex (Saqqara)}

\textbf{Overview:}  
The ancient Step Pyramid of Djoser, surrounded by labyrinthine catacombs.

\textbf{Portal:}  
Granite slab in the Serapeum catacombs, quantum-locked.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Survival/Investigation roll (DC 15) to navigate the labyrinth and find the portal.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Avoid traps and solve a labyrinth riddle to power the door.

\textbf{Success:}  
Ancient tech and a map fragment to the "ocean pyramids."

\textbf{Failure:}  
Trap triggers or get lost in the catacombs.

\textbf{Revelation:}  
Map fragment hints at the Osireion: "Where water flows, the old gods whisper."
