\section*{Site: Step Pyramid Complex (Saqqara)}

\textbf{Overview:}  
The ancient Step Pyramid of Djoser, surrounded by labyrinthine catacombs.

\textbf{Portal:}  
Granite slab in the Serapeum catacombs, quantum-locked.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Survival/Investigation roll (DC 15) to navigate the labyrinth and find the portal.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Avoid traps and solve a labyrinth riddle to power the door.

\textbf{Success:}  
Ancient tech and a map fragment to the "ocean pyramids." Find an Atlantean battery.

\textbf{Failure:}  
Trap triggers or get lost in the catacombs.

\textbf{Item Drops:}  
Atlantean battery, navigation crystal, fragment of a key.

\textbf{Revelations:}
- "Where water flows, the old gods whisper (hint: Osireion)."
- "You will need three keys, three batteries, and three messages to reach the final library."
- "A vision of a face on a distant red world (hint: Mars)."
- "The rites of three lands are required for the last passage."
