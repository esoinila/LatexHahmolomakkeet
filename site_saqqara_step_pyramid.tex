\textbf{Site: Step Pyramid Complex (Saqqara)}

\textbf{Overview:}  
The ancient Step Pyramid of Djoser, surrounded by labyrinthine catacombs.

\textbf{Portal:}  
Granite slab in the Serapeum catacombs, quantum-locked.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Survival/Investigation roll (DC 15) to navigate the labyrinth and find the portal.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Avoid traps and solve a labyrinth riddle to power the door.

\textbf{Success:}  
Ancient tech and a map fragment to the "ocean pyramids." Find an Atlantean battery.

\textbf{Failure:}  
Trap triggers or get lost in the catacombs.

\textbf{Item Drops:}  
Atlantean battery, navigation crystal, fragment of a key.

\textbf{Revelations:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Where the life-giving waters flow and the ancient currents speak, a forgotten wisdom lies submerged, awaiting discovery.
  \item The passage to the final truth is barred until the three sacred lights and the three vessels of primal energy are united.
  \item Gaze upon the crimson sphere in the night sky; its silent face points the way to a mountain of mysteries, where the next key is held.
\end{itemize}
