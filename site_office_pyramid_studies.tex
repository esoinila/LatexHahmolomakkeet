\section*{Site: Office of Pyramid Studies}

\textbf{Overview:}  
A modern research office filled with ancient secrets and high-tech security.

\textbf{Portal:}  
A hidden safe with an Atlantean lock, requiring special keys (found in other sites) and power from Kate/Karl.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Investigation/Tech roll (DC 14) to find the safe and decipher research notes.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Players must assemble the keys and input the correct code (hinted in the notes).

\textbf{Success:}  
Safe opens, revealing an Atlantean artifact and a map to a lost chamber.

\textbf{Failure:}  
Modern security triggers (alarm, guards, or lockdown).

\textbf{Revelation:}  
Clue to the next ancient site: "Where the builders once rested, secrets remain."
