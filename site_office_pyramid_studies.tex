\section*{Site: Office of Pyramid Studies}

\textbf{Overview:}  
A modern research office filled with ancient secrets and high-tech security.

\textbf{Portal:}  
A hidden safe with an Atlantean lock, requiring special keys (found in other sites) and power from Kate/Karl.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Investigation/Tech roll (DC 14) to find the safe and decipher research notes.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Players must assemble the keys and input the correct code (hinted in the notes).

\textbf{Success:}  
Safe opens, revealing an Atlantean artifact and a map to a lost chamber.

\textbf{Failure:}  
Modern security triggers (alarm, guards, or lockdown).

\textbf{Revelations:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Where the hands that shaped the ancient stones found their rest, there lies a fragment of the forgotten knowledge.
  \item The path to the hidden heart demands the convergence of three luminous keys and three vessels charged with the ancestral power, scattered across the lands.
  \item The whispers of the deep currents guide the way to the submerged city. Find its heart, and the path to the Architect's library will be revealed.
\end{itemize}
