\textbf{Site: The Great Sphinx}

\textbf{Overview:}  
The enigmatic guardian of Giza, rumored to hide many secrets.

\textbf{Portal:}  
A stone panel between the Sphinx's paws, with a faded Atlantean eye.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Arcana/Insight roll (DC 16) to sense the portal's energy.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Players must answer the Sphinx's riddle (customize for your game).

\textbf{Success:}  
Panel slides open, revealing a deep shaft.  
- Message: "You stand at the threshold of Atlantis. Only the worthy may proceed."  
- Tech: a voice modulator (lets players speak ancient languages).

\textbf{Failure:}  
Sphinx's roar stuns players (lose a turn or take minor damage).

\textbf{Revelations:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item At the threshold of the sleeping guardian, the final path unfolds. Seek the wisdom held within the heart of the great pyramid, where the Architect's library awaits.
  \item The ancient pact requires the convergence of three luminous keys and three vessels charged with the primal force. Gather them from the scattered sites to unlock the ultimate truth.
  \item The echoes of the sunken city resonate through stone. Find its submerged heart, and the whispers of the deep will guide you through the veil.
\end{itemize}
