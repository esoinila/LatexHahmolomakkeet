\section*{Site: The Great Sphinx}

\textbf{Overview:}  
The enigmatic guardian of Giza, rumored to hide many secrets.

\textbf{Portal:}  
A stone panel between the Sphinx's paws, with a faded Atlantean eye.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Arcana/Insight roll (DC 16) to sense the portal's energy.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Players must answer the Sphinx's riddle (customize for your game).

\textbf{Success:}  
Panel slides open, revealing a deep shaft.  
- Message: "You stand at the threshold of Atlantis. Only the worthy may proceed."  
- Tech: a voice modulator (lets players speak ancient languages).

\textbf{Failure:}  
Sphinx's roar stuns players (lose a turn or take minor damage).

\textbf{Revelation:}  
Final clue to the Atlantean gateway beneath the Pyramid of Khufu.
