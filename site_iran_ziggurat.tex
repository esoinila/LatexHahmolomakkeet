\section*{Site: Ancient Ziggurat (Iran)}

\textbf{Overview:}  
The Chogha Zanbil ziggurat, a massive ancient structure with hidden chambers.

\textbf{Portal:}  
Granite door hidden in a collapsed chamber, quantum-locked.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Stealth/History roll (DC 16) to avoid detection and find the portal.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Solve a cuneiform puzzle and power the door.

\textbf{Success:}  
Ancient Sumerian tech and a warning about "the deep."

\textbf{Failure:}  
Authorities or secret organizations intervene.

\textbf{Revelation:}  
Warning about Eridu: "Where the first city sleeps, danger stirs."
