\section*{Site: Ancient Ziggurat (Iran)}

\textbf{Overview:}  
The Chogha Zanbil ziggurat, a massive ancient structure with hidden chambers.

\textbf{Portal:}  
Granite door hidden in a collapsed chamber, quantum-locked.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Stealth/History roll (DC 16) to avoid detection and find the portal.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Solve a cuneiform puzzle and power the door.

\textbf{Success:}  
Ancient Sumerian tech and a warning about "the deep."

\textbf{Failure:}  
Authorities or secret organizations intervene.

\textbf{Item Drops:}  
Atlantean key, Sumerian battery, navigation crystal.

\textbf{Revelations:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Where the dust of the first city settles, danger slumbers. The path forward lies where the most ancient stones whisper secrets of the deep.
  \item The final wisdom is veiled until three luminous keys and three vessels of ancestral power are gathered from across the lands, and the echoes of the three sacred rites are heard.
  \item A vision granted by the ancient ones reveals a hidden passage within the fortified halls of a ruler's domain, a step towards the ultimate goal.
\end{itemize}
