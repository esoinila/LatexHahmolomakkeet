## Background
Enheduanna was born in the settlements near the ruins of the Great Museum. While others scavenged for scrap metal, she scavenged for history. She believes that the fall of the old world was due to forgetting the lessons of the past. By taking the name of the ancient poet, she embodies the bridge between the forgotten golden age and the harsh present, hoping to restore culture alongside survival.
