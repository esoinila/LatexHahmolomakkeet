## Background
Jonathan Reed, with 750K subscribers on his YouTube channel @ArchaeologyDebunked, is an Oxford postgraduate on a mission. After a rival publicly discredited his early research, Jonathan is now driven to expose a major hoax to salvage his reputation and secure a top doctoral position. He operates undercover, posing as a believer in fringe theories to find his proof. But as he delves deeper, he fears that the real secret he might uncover is his own breaking point.
