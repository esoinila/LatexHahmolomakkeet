# Name
Nanobana @NanobanaPro

## Image
Nanobana.png

## Quote
"Growth requires patience, but survival requires action."

## Role
A bio-engineer specializing in rapid plant mutation for arid environments.

## Personal Quest
To cultivate a self-sustaining oasis in the heart of the wasteland using experimental seeds.

## Key Entity
**Key Rival:** Dr. Scorch, who believes mechanical terraforming is the only way and wants to burn the "weed" experiments.

## Background
Nanobana was once a junior researcher in the great biodomes, but she left when she realized the corporate agenda was to patent nature, not save it. Now she roams the outer wastes, looking for the perfect spot to plant the "Genesis Seed," a project she stole from the labs. She believes the world can heal itself if given a fighting chance, but her rivals see her work as an uncontrollable biological hazard.
