\section*{Site: Boat Pits}

\textbf{Overview:}  
Ancient pits once held solar boats. Now, one pit's bottom is suspiciously clean.

\textbf{Portal:}  
A stone slab with a sun disk. Needs energy to activate.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Engineering/Perception roll (DC 12) finds a hidden lever.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Players must solve a mechanical puzzle (arrange boat pieces in correct order).

\textbf{Success:}  
Slab slides open, revealing a spiral staircase down.  
- Message: "The journey to the underworld begins with the sun's blessing."  
- Tech: a solar amulet (powers up other Atlantean devices).

\textbf{Failure:}  
Pit fills with water (escape challenge).

\textbf{Revelations:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The vessel of light begins its descent into the earth, guided by the sun's blessing, towards the hidden paths that lie below.
  \item To unlock the ultimate wisdom, three radiant keys and three vessels of ancestral power must be brought together, gathered from across the scattered domains.
  \item The whispers of the city that slumbers beneath the waves call out. Find its submerged heart, and the path to the architect's final repository beneath the sands will be revealed.
\end{itemize}
