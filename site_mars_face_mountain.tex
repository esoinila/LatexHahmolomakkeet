\section*{Site: Face Mountain (Mars)}

\textbf{Overview:}  
A colossal, face-shaped mountain on Mars, hiding an ancient Atlantean outpost beneath its surface. The air is thin, gravity is low, and the tech is alien.

\textbf{Portal:}  
A seamless granite door embedded in the mountain's "mouth," quantum-locked. Requires a battery and a navigation crystal to activate.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Science/Arcana roll (DC 18) to find the door and decipher Martian glyphs. Avoid detection by automated sentries.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Solve a holographic star-map puzzle and synchronize the portal with Earth. Power the door with a battery.

\textbf{Success:}  
Access to the Martian outpost, find an Atlantean key, and a message from the "Red Watchers." Gain a vision of the final Giza repository.

\textbf{Failure:}  
Sentries activate, or the portal destabilizes (risk of being stranded on Mars).

\textbf{Item Drops:}  
Atlantean key, Martian battery, fragment of a navigation map.

\textbf{Revelations:}
- "The red star rises—one key is found, but two more are needed."
- "You will need three keys, three batteries, and three messages to reach the final library."
- "A vision of a sunken city beneath the waves (hint: ocean Giza clone)."
- "The rites of three lands are required for the last passage."
- "The Watchers warn: 'Do not awaken what sleeps beneath the sand.'"
