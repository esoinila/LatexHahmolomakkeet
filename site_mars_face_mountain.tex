\textbf{Site: Face Mountain (Mars)}

\textbf{Overview:}  
A colossal, face-shaped mountain on Mars, hiding an ancient Atlantean outpost beneath its surface. The air is thin, gravity is low, and the tech is alien.

\textbf{Portal:}  
A seamless granite door embedded in the mountain's "mouth," quantum-locked. Requires a battery and a navigation crystal to activate.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Science/Arcana roll (DC 18) to find the door and decipher Martian glyphs. Avoid detection by automated sentries.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Solve a holographic star-map puzzle and synchronize the portal with Earth. Power the door with a battery.

\textbf{Success:}  
Access to the Martian outpost, find an Atlantean key, and a message from the "Red Watchers." Gain a vision of the final Giza repository.

\textbf{Failure:}  
Sentries activate, or the portal destabilizes (risk of being stranded on Mars).

\textbf{Item Drops:}  
Atlantean key, Martian battery, fragment of a navigation map.

\textbf{Revelations:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item From the humming gateway, a voice echoes across the gulf of space and time: To pass the final threshold, three radiant keys and three vessels of ancient energy must be gathered, and the wisdom of those who walked the three sacred lands must be understood.
  \item A vision of the enlightened one, Toth, emerges, his eyes alight with cosmic knowledge: The stone mountain must ascend once more, or the tides of oblivion will claim all that is.
  \item The very stone of this place shimmers, revealing hidden truths: A dormant power source lies waiting, to be awakened by a vessel of energy. Seek the city that slumbers beneath the waves, but heed the warnings of the deep.
\end{itemize}
