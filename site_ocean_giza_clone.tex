\section*{Site: Sunken Giza Clone (Atlantic Ocean, west of Cuba)}

\textbf{Overview:}  
Three pyramids under the ocean, protected by a force field that keeps the water out while power lasts.

\textbf{Portal:}  
Granite door in the central pyramid, only open while power lasts. Quantum-locked.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Survival/Arcana roll (DC 18) to survive the pressure and find the portal.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Power the door and avoid Atlantean guardians.

\textbf{Success:}  
Glimpse of the "original" civilization and a dire warning.

\textbf{Failure:}  
Force field weakens, water begins to seep in.

\textbf{Revelation:}  
Warning: "The cycle repeats. Only the worthy may return."
