\section*{Site: Sunken Giza Clone (Atlantic Ocean, west of Cuba)}

\textbf{Overview:}  
Three pyramids under the ocean, protected by a force field that keeps the water out while power lasts.

\textbf{Portal:}  
Granite door in the central pyramid, only open while power lasts. Quantum-locked.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Survival/Arcana roll (DC 18) to survive the pressure and find the portal.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Power the door and avoid Atlantean guardians.

\textbf{Success:}  
Glimpse of the "original" civilization and a dire warning.

\textbf{Failure:}  
Force field weakens, water begins to seep in.

\textbf{Item Drops:}  
Atlantean key, ocean battery, navigation crystal.

\textbf{Revelations:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The great cycle turns, as it has since the dawn of time. Only those whose spirits are worthy and whose hands bear the tokens may return to the source.
  \item To pierce the final veil and stand before the Architect's ultimate truth, three radiant keys must align with three vessels of life's spark, woven together with the ancient whispers.
  \item From the depths where stone cities dream, a vision surfaces: a hidden door within the keepers' archive, a gateway to a past they sought to bury.
\end{itemize}
