\section*{Site: Sphinx's Shadow Chamber (Giza)}

\textbf{Overview:}  
A hidden chamber beneath the Sphinx, only accessible at dawn when the shadow falls just right.

\textbf{Portal:}  
Granite door in the bedrock, visible only at a certain time. Quantum-locked, requires social media energy.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Timing/Perception roll (DC 15) to find the door at the right moment.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Avoid Sphinx guardians and solve a time-based riddle to activate the portal.

\textbf{Success:}  
Ancient message about the "world gates" and a key for long-distance portals.

\textbf{Failure:}  
Guardian encounter or missed timing (must wait another day).

\textbf{Revelation:}  
Clue to the next portal: "Where queens weep, the path continues."
