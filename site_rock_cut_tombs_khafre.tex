\section*{Site: Rock Cut Tombs (Khafre area)}

\textbf{Overview:}  
Tombs carved into the rock near Khafre's pyramid, decorated with sun and serpent motifs.

\textbf{Portal:}  
A carved door with sun and serpent motifs, powered by Kate/Karl.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Survival/Insight roll (DC 14) to find the door and interpret the carvings.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Survive a trap gauntlet and interpret the wall carvings to unlock the portal.

\textbf{Success:}  
Solar medallion, prophecy about the Sphinx, and the final portal key.

\textbf{Failure:}  
Trap triggers (poison darts, falling rocks, etc.).

\textbf{Revelations:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The great stone beast, the silent guardian, waits for those who understand the ancient symbols of light and transformation.
  \item The path to the ultimate wisdom is sealed, requiring the union of three radiant keys and three vessels filled with ancestral power.
  \item Seek the submerged heart of the forgotten city, where the deep currents hum with the secrets of the cosmos and reveal the way to the hidden repository.
\end{itemize}
