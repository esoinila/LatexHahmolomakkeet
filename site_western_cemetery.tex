\textbf{Site: Western Cemetery}

\textbf{Overview:}  
A vast field of mastabas west of the Great Pyramid, home to ancient priests and nobles.

\textbf{Portal:}  
An ancient mastaba with a false wall, marked by faded Atlantean glyphs.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Perception/History roll (DC 13) to spot the glyphs and hidden wall.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Solve a riddle left by a high priest (hint: "What walks on four legs in the morning...").

\textbf{Success:}  
Wall opens to a secret chamber. Message from an ancient architect and a piece of a portal key.

\textbf{Failure:}  
A sand trap or spectral priest appears.

\textbf{Revelations:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Where hands shaped stone, the next key lies.
  \item The echoes of the ancients whisper of three lights and three vessels, hidden across the lands, essential to bridge the void.
  \item The final journey demands the wisdom of stars and the strength of stone. Seek the heart that beats beneath the sands, where the path to the great library is revealed.
\end{itemize}
