\section*{Site: Western Cemetery}

\textbf{Overview:}  
A vast field of mastabas west of the Great Pyramid, home to ancient priests and nobles.

\textbf{Portal:}  
An ancient mastaba with a false wall, marked by faded Atlantean glyphs.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Perception/History roll (DC 13) to spot the glyphs and hidden wall.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Solve a riddle left by a high priest (hint: "What walks on four legs in the morning...").

\textbf{Success:}  
Wall opens to a secret chamber. Message from an ancient architect and a piece of a portal key.

\textbf{Failure:}  
A sand trap or spectral priest appears.

\textbf{Revelation:}  
Architect's message hints at the Builders' Quarters: "Where hands shaped stone, the next key lies."
