\section*{Site: Valley of the Queens (Giza)}

\textbf{Overview:}  
A field of lesser-known tombs south of the main pyramids, shrouded in mystery.

\textbf{Portal:}  
Granite door disguised as a sarcophagus lid, quantum-locked.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
History/Perception roll (DC 14) to spot the disguised portal.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Decipher the Queen's lament and power the door while avoiding curses.

\textbf{Success:}  
Vision of a distant land (foreshadowing Andes or Eridu) and a portal key. Find an Atlantean battery.

\textbf{Failure:}  
Curse or spectral queen appears.

\textbf{Item Drops:}  
Atlantean battery, fragment of an ancient message.

\textbf{Revelations:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item From the place where stone stairs ascend to touch the sky, profound secrets lie hidden below.
  \item The ancient path is sealed until three lights are gathered and three vessels of power are joined with three whispers of forgotten lore, unlocking the gate to the final wisdom.
  \item A vision arises from the mists of time: a city submerged, where the deep currents guard a key to the stars.
\end{itemize}
