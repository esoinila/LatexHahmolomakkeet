\section*{Site: Valley of the Queens (Giza)}

\textbf{Overview:}  
A field of lesser-known tombs south of the main pyramids, shrouded in mystery.

\textbf{Portal:}  
Granite door disguised as a sarcophagus lid, quantum-locked.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
History/Perception roll (DC 14) to spot the disguised portal.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Decipher the Queen's lament and power the door while avoiding curses.

\textbf{Success:}  
Vision of a distant land (foreshadowing Andes or Eridu) and a portal key.

\textbf{Failure:}  
Curse or spectral queen appears.

\textbf{Revelation:}  
Vision hints at the Step Pyramid: "Where steps rise, secrets descend."
