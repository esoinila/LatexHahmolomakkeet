\textbf{Site: Tomb of Queen Khentkawes}

\textbf{Overview:}  
A mysterious tomb with star motifs and hidden dangers.

\textbf{Portal:}  
A sarcophagus with a hidden Atlantean mechanism, powered by Kate/Karl.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Arcana/Perception roll (DC 15) to find the mechanism and star symbols.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Align the star symbols in the correct order to unlock the sarcophagus.

\textbf{Success:}  
Vision from the Queen, ancient password, and a piece of the final key.

\textbf{Failure:}  
Curses or spectral guardians attack.

\textbf{Revelations:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Where celestial patterns merge with earthly forms, a hidden passage lies. Seek the place where the light of the sun embraces the wisdom of the serpent.
  \item The path to ancient truth is sealed until three luminous keys and three vessels brimming with ancestral power are brought together.
  \item The echoes of the submerged city call out. Find its heart, hidden beneath the currents, and it will illuminate the way to the architect's final resting place.
\end{itemize}
