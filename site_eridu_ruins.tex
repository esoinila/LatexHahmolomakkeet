\section*{Site: Eridu Ruins (Iraq)}

\textbf{Overview:}  
The abandoned city of Eridu, shrouded in sand and secrecy.

\textbf{Portal:}  
Granite door in a buried temple, quantum-locked and guarded.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Stealth/Arcana roll (DC 17) to avoid patrols and find the portal.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Decipher Sumerian prophecy and power the door.

\textbf{Success:}  
Message from the "first city" and a clue to the ocean pyramids.

\textbf{Failure:}  
Military or secret organizations intervene.

\textbf{Item Drops:}  
Atlantean key, Eridu battery, navigation crystal.

\textbf{Revelations:}
- "Where the ocean hides the old ones, the journey ends (hint: ocean Giza clone)."
- "You will need three keys, three batteries, and three messages to reach the final library."
- "A vision of a hidden door in a secret military base."
