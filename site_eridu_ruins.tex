\section*{Site: Eridu Ruins (Iraq)}

\textbf{Overview:}  
The abandoned city of Eridu, shrouded in sand and secrecy.

\textbf{Portal:}  
Granite door in a buried temple, quantum-locked and guarded.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Stealth/Arcana roll (DC 17) to avoid patrols and find the portal.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Decipher Sumerian prophecy and power the door.

\textbf{Success:}  
Message from the "first city" and a clue to the ocean pyramids.

\textbf{Failure:}  
Military or secret organizations intervene.

\textbf{Item Drops:}  
Atlantean key, Eridu battery, navigation crystal.

\textbf{Revelations:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Where the vast waters embrace the wisdom of the ancient ones, the path to the journey's end is concealed within the depths.
  \item The final passage to the architect's truth is sealed until the three radiant keys and three vessels brimming with primal energy are gathered from across the scattered domains.
  \item A vision arises from the dust of the first city: a hidden entrance guarded by the instruments of war, a step closer to the ultimate goal.
\end{itemize}
