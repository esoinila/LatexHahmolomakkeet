\section*{Site: Osireion (Abydos)}

\textbf{Overview:}  
A mysterious underground temple, half-submerged and filled with ancient energy.

\textbf{Portal:}  
Granite door with "flower of life" motif, quantum-locked and half-submerged.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Arcana/Survival roll (DC 16) to find and access the portal.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Solve a water puzzle and commune with a spirit to power the door.

\textbf{Success:}  
Clue to the "original" pyramids and a long-distance portal key. Find an Atlantean battery.

\textbf{Failure:}  
Spirit's wrath or flooding.

\textbf{Item Drops:}  
Atlantean battery, fragment of a key, navigation crystal.

\textbf{Revelations:}
- "Where the sun rises over the lake, a gate awaits (hint: Andes)."
- "You will need three keys, three batteries, and three messages to reach the final library."
- "A vision of a hidden door in a dictator's palace."
- "The rites of three lands are required for the last passage."
