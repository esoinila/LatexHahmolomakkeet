\section*{Site: Dendera Temple (Egypt)}

\textbf{Overview:}  
The Temple of Hathor at Dendera, famous for its mysterious reliefs and hidden chambers.

\textbf{Portal:}  
Granite door behind the "Dendera light" relief, quantum-locked.

\textbf{Discovery:}  
Arcana/Perception roll (DC 15) to find the portal and decipher the relief.

\textbf{Puzzle:}  
Solve a light-based puzzle and power the door while avoiding temple priests.

\textbf{Success:}  
Ancient energy device and a vision of the "world grid."

\textbf{Failure:}  
Priests intervene or puzzle triggers a trap.

\textbf{Revelation:}  
Vision: "The grid connects all, but beware the watchers."
